## � Data & Privacy

The application uses browser `localStorage` to save your learning progress and preferences.

- Your saved data is associated with your browser and website origin.
- Your progress does not automatically sync between devices or browsers.
- Clearing browser data may erase your saved progress.
- Export backups regularly to avoid losing your work.
- Never store passwords, API secrets, or other sensitive information in local storage.

Third-party resources may require an internet connection.
